\section{Mathematical Typesetting (`amsmath`)}
LaTeX provides unmatched precision for equations, supporting both inline expressions ($E = mc^2$) and prominent display environments.

\begin{equation}
    f(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \hat{f}(\xi) \, e^{2\pi i \xi x} \, d\xi
\end{equation}

Furthermore, matrices and systems of equations are constructed cleanly using alignment arrays:
\begin{equation}
    A = \begin{bmatrix} 
        1 & 2 & 3 \\ 
        4 & 5 & 6 \\ 
        7 & 8 & 9 
    \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}
